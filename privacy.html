Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Short version

NEXUS does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Everything you do in the app stays in your browser's local storage on your device.

What the app stores

  • Your XP, level, training streak, and drill history.
  • Your sensitivity settings, saved loadouts, and cleared bosses.
  • Your sound and animation preferences.

This data is written to localStorage under the key nexus.progress.v1. It never leaves your device, is not uploaded to a server, and is not linked to an account or identity.

No accounts, analytics, or ads

NEXUS has no login, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracking. The app works offline after the first load. Fonts and icons are bundled locally.

Installing and the Play Store

When installed from a store or added to your home screen, the platform may process standard install diagnostics under its own privacy policy. NEXUS itself does not receive that data.

Deleting your data

Open the Operator profile and choose Reset all progress to erase local data. Clearing the app's site data or uninstalling the app has the same effect. Because no data is stored on a server, no remote deletion request is needed.

Children

The app is a general-audience gaming utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Game names and trademarks

NEXUS is an independent, fan-made companion. Game names, boss names, and related marks belong to their respective publishers. NEXUS is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any game publisher. Sensitivity constants and loadout statistics are community guidance and may change with game updates.
